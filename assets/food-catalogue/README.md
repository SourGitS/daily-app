# Food catalogue artwork

Original generated raster artwork for the v379 Food catalogue campaign, selected and
developed with Francois. The direction is light editorial food photography with warm paper,
black typography and blue collage accents. The three guidance icons use tactile sculptural
objects in the same palette. Food depictions are illustrations, not photographs of tested
recipe outcomes; the catalogue identifies them as illustrations.

- `birria.webp`: the announcement cover and catalogue entry image.
- `collection.webp`: an exact 3-column × 2-row contact sheet, displayed with CSS positioning.
  Order: Birria, Cajun Alfredo, Parmentier; Lasagne, Tuscan Salmon, Char Siu Chicken.
- `icons.webp`: a 3-column strip of Browse recipes, Cook with steps and Shopping illustrations.

Generated PNG masters were retained in the creating chat's generated-images directory.
These WebP assets are the complete runtime exports; all wording is accessible HTML, never
baked into the images. Blue belongs to this campaign and does not change the user's accent.
